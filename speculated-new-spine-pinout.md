# Speculation about the 4 pin + USB spine

The newer models feature a 4 pin spine. 

I have spotted some excellent photos on an ebay listing which show:
* The USB section in the spine has pads inside (the earlier spine is only the shield)
* The power input is listed as 48v DC at 0.2A.
* The phone side of the KEM has a real USB port with pads too (the earlier version is only a friction/ground connector for the shield)

## On the phone side

The phone has the pins labelled as 1 at the bottom (base of the phone) and 4 at the top of the phone (screen/usb port)
The middle 2 pins (of the 4 on the spine) on the phone are connected to the isolated area of the power.
* The top most middle pin (3) seems to be the power (connected to 1 end of a diode (and?) capacitor)
* The lower most middle pin (2) seems to be the negative (connectivity to quite a few of the other components)


The outer pins (1 and 4) are connected to a nearby cluster of filters/capacitors/resistor. They appear to be for device discovery/identification.
